## 🧠 Core Algorithmic & Behavioral Innovations

### 1. 🔥 RankBrain Pogo-Sticking Algorithm (`behavior/pogo_sticking.py`)
Simulates the highest-impact Google ranking satisfaction signal. The worker searches on Google/Bing, clicks a top competitor result, stays 3–7 seconds in discontent, clicks Back to SERP, and engages deeply on your target site for 2–5 minutes. This sends an unmistakable signal to Google's RankBrain that your website resolved user intent far better than competitors.

### 2. ⏱ Circadian Dayparting Curve (`behavior/dayparting.py`)
Simulates authentic 24-hour human activity curves using a bimodal Gaussian distribution (11:30 AM lunch research peak, 8:45 PM evening research peak, and 3:00–5:00 AM sleep lull). Completely prevents the flat 24/7 robotic traffic line that flags Google Analytics 4 anomaly detection.

### 3. 📱 Mobile Device Matrix & Touch Emulation (`behavior/device_matrix.py`)
Over 60% of organic Google searches happen on mobile devices. OrbitTraffic emulates genuine iPhone 15 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S24, and Pixel 8 touch viewports with real `TouchEvent` APIs, device pixel ratios, orientation handling, and mobile Client Hints (`Sec-CH-UA-Mobile: ?1`).

### 4. 🧭 Multi-Hop Organic Journey Engine (`behavior/journey.py`)
Eliminates unnatural single-page bounces. Workers dynamically discover internal links (`/blog`, `/about`, `/pricing`, related products) and browse 1 to 4 pages per session with authentic dwell times and reading speeds.

### 5. 🍪 Persona & Cookie Pre-Warming Farm (`browser/persona.py`)
Pre-warms personas by automatically visiting authoritative domains (Wikipedia, News, Reddit) to acquire legitimate third-party analytics and ad cookies before executing campaign targets.

### 6. 📊 Executive Client PDF/CSV Report Exporter (`analytics/report_generator.py`)
Exports client-ready CSV and JSON audit reports tracking verified impressions, dwell time retention, hops completed, and Pogo-Sticking executions.
